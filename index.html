A letter you can keep

Read this slowly, princess. Every word is meant for you.

✧ ♡ ✧
My dearest Upangsha,

Happy Princess Day, my love.

I wish I could be beside you right now, hold your hand, look into your eyes, and tell you all of this without a screen between us. Until that day, let this little website carry my words to you.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for accepting me even on the days when I don't feel like my best self. I love the way you smile, the way you can be so mature and caring and then suddenly act like the cutest baby. You make me laugh, you make me feel understood, and you make distance feel a little less difficult.

I don't promise that every day will be perfect. I promise to keep choosing honesty, effort, respect, and us. I want to collect ordinary days with you, explore new places together, cook the food you crave, and one day build that little cottage in the mountains where we can watch the world slow down.

Until then, please remember this: you don't need to do anything extraordinary to be precious to me. You already are. Not just today, not just on Princess Day — on the random Tuesdays, the difficult days, and every little day in between.

Keep smiling, my princess. Your smile is one of my favourite places in this world.

Forever cheering for you,
your favourite human ♡
